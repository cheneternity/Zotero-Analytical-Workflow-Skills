# Synthesis Maturity Gate

Creating a Theme does not automatically create a Synthesis page. Re-evaluate Synthesis only after the theme has:

- multiple stable cross-paper Relations or Claims;
- enough cross-paper evidence to support a genuine synthesis rather than a paper list; and
- at least one synthesis-worthy condition, mechanism, boundary, controversy, or evidence-backed gap.

Do not use a rigid paper count as the sole gate. A Theme may exist while its Synthesis remains immature. Preserve the existing Theme and Synthesis creation gates, and record `NO_SYNTHESIS` when the maturity evidence is not yet present.
